OpenChasqui now ships three packages. The CLI bundles the pipeline and every built-in plugin, `plugin-kit` also carries the plugin contract and the canonical model that lived in `openchasqui-spec`, and `data-es-pe` is unchanged. The other nine packages are no longer published; import their types from `@abitechdev/openchasqui-plugin-kit`.
